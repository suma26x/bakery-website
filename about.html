OUR STORY

From a Small Dream to a Sweet Tradition

Baking memories since day one.

Suma's Vintage Bakes began with a simple dream — to create a bakery where every customer could experience the warmth of homemade baking.

Inspired by traditional recipes and the charm of old-fashioned bakeries, we carefully prepare breads, pastries, cakes and desserts using quality ingredients.

What started as a small passion for baking has grown into a place where families, friends and celebrations come together.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE DREAM

Meet Suma

"Every recipe tells a story."

Suma's love for baking began with a fascination for traditional homemade desserts and the joy they brought to family gatherings.

Her vision was to create a bakery that combines the elegance of a vintage bakehouse with the creativity of modern pastry making.

Today, that vision lives on through every loaf, pastry and celebration cake prepared at Suma's Vintage Bakes.
